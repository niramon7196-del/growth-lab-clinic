## GLOBAL BRAND ASSET LOCK

DO NOT MODIFY THE GLOBAL GROWTH LAB LOGO UNDER ANY CIRCUMSTANCE. Treat it as a protected brand asset. Any future layout adjustment must preserve the exact same logo asset, artwork, colors, transparency, aspect ratio, and identity. Fix surrounding layout instead of modifying the logo.

### Strict Rules for GROWTH LAB Official Logo:
- **Global Locked Asset**: The GROWTH LAB Official Logo (`src/components/Logo.tsx`) is a protected master brand asset across all application views (Sidebar, Landing Page, Login, Dashboard, Patient Views, Mobile Drawer, Reports).
- **No Variations or Re-creation**: Never create new logos, SVG variants, or placeholder artwork.
- **No Background Cards**: Never enclose or add solid white cards, white boxes, or opaque backgrounds behind the logo. It must render seamlessly with transparency.
- **No Alteration**: Never change the logo's colors, artwork, transparency, or aspect ratio.
- **Responsive Layout Adjustments**: If space is constrained on any breakpoint, adjust surrounding containers, padding, margins, or responsive grid scaling — NEVER modify the logo artwork itself.
- **Unified Master Component**: All components and screens MUST reference the single master `Logo` component.
